Projects

Integrated Database and LLM for Clinical Decision SupportDepartment Prize

MSc dissertation (Distinction, 81/100) and a feasibility study: a clinical decision-support system where a local LLM (Qwen2.5) explains a machine-learning model's results using NICE NG101 guideline text. I harmonised three datasets (10,024 records) into one schema and trained a classifier that reached 86.73% accuracy and 0.742 macro-F1 under 5-fold cross-validation, then applied it to a separate 992-patient dataset without retraining. An ablation showed data availability mattered 2.4 to 4.5 times more than model architecture. SHAP and LIME supported the explanations. Early versions hallucinated in 6 of 30 explanations (20%); redesigning the system so my code states the facts and the LLM only summarises them brought that to 0. I worked with clinical experts to define the model inputs and presented the results to clinicians and non-technical audiences. Awarded the Department of Computer Science prize for the best project in the 2025/26 MSc Data Science cohort.
